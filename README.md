# VOID

A top-down arcade survival shooter. You are the UFO. It's Earth, year 41,207,
and humanity grew up on stories about ships like yours. Endless waves of
cyberpunk humans, a boss every 10 waves. Dark galaxy, gore aesthetic.

**▶ Play:** https://r0ck574r.github.io/Void/

## Features
- Nine human enemy types (Slicer, Brute, Overclock, Handler, Ronin, Enforcer, Carrier, Skitter, Sniper) plus elites
- Two bosses: The Heart every 10 waves, The Hollow Sovereign every 20, with its own synthesized score
- Augment drafts every 5 waves, fire-rate level-ups every 10
- Three zones that shift every 20 waves
- Daily run: the same seeded waves and augment offers for everyone, each day
- A worried UFO whose bloodshot eyes stare wherever you shoot
- Soft shadows and textured surfaces throughout

## Controls
- WASD / Arrows — Move
- Mouse — Aim & auto-fire
- Space — Pulse blast
- 1 / 2 / 3 — Pick an augment
- P / Esc — Pause · M — Sound · R — Restart
- Phone — Drag to move, aim is automatic, tap PULSE

## Tech
Single-file HTML5 canvas. No dependencies, no build step.
Synthesized audio via Web Audio API.
